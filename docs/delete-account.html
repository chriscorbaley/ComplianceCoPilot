What deleting your account does

Deleting your Compliance Co-Pilot account permanently removes the account and everything stored in it. This includes:

  • Your sign-in and account profile
  • All compliance documents and uploaded files
  • Business trip records, hours logs, and meeting minutes
  • Mileage records and vehicles
  • Businesses, properties, and signed records

This cannot be undone. Once the deletion is confirmed it happens immediately, and we have no way to recover your account or any of your records afterwards.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription. Only the store can stop the billing, so please cancel it there as well if you have a paid plan.
